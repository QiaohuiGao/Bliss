Design rationale

Conversation with a visible outcome

The interface answers four questions at all times: Where are we in the wedding? What are we solving now? What can Bliss do? What becomes real after this conversation?

1 · Wedding journey

The 14 chapters stay visible as one connected story without exposing a giant checklist.

2 · Current chapter

The left rail combines local progress with specific capabilities, so users know exactly how Bliss can help.

3 · Agent conversation

Natural dialogue remains central, but attributed preferences and sourced comparisons appear as structured conversational objects.

4 · Decision dock

Decisions, approvals, and Moments live outside the chat stream. They are reviewable, deterministic, and persistent.

5 · The shared thread

Violet and coral represent two individual voices. They meet only where the couple has shared understanding or confirmation.

6 · Progressive disclosure

One next decision and one approval are prominent. Deeper planning remains available without becoming visual noise.
